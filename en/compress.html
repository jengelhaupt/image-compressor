Compress Images Online – Save Storage Space and Still Shrink Your Images!

Large image files slow down websites, take up unnecessary storage space and make it harder to share by email or messenger. With the right compression you can make images much smaller without the quality visibly suffering. Whether JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF – optimized file sizes give you faster loading times, better performance and more efficient storage. Modern compression methods reduce data intelligently while keeping the most important image information.

JPG: The most common image format – compress it online now

Compress JPG your way under 100 KB under 250 KB resize multiple JPGs

The JPG format (JPEG) is one of the most widely used image formats on the internet. It is particularly well suited for photos and complex images with many colors and gradients. In JPG compression, image information is reduced by merging details that are barely noticeable to the human eye. Lossy compression makes very small file sizes possible. Depending on the quality level you choose, you decide how much the file is reduced. JPG is ideal for websites, blogs or online shops because it offers an excellent balance between image quality and file size.

PNG: Shrink your logos, icons or graphics quite easily with our compressor

Compress PNG your way with transparency

PNG is especially suitable for graphics, logos, icons or images with transparency. Unlike JPG, PNG works losslessly by default. That means no image information is permanently removed during compression. PNG compression optimizes the file structure and removes unnecessary metadata, so the image quality is fully preserved. Especially with clear lines, text or illustrations, PNG delivers razor-sharp results. If you need transparency or the highest level of detail matters, PNG is the right choice.

WebP: Use the most modern image format with ease

Compress WebP your way

WebP is a modern image format developed specifically for the web. It combines the advantages of JPG and PNG and allows both lossy and lossless compression. Compared to JPG or PNG, WebP files are often significantly smaller – at comparable or even better quality. This noticeably improves loading times, which has a positive effect on user experience and search engine ranking. WebP also supports transparency and is excellent for modern websites that value performance.

Compress PDFs for faster sending and archiving

PDFs can also quickly become very large – especially when they contain many images or graphics. Targeted PDF compression optimizes embedded images, removes unnecessary data and reduces the file size. The result is smaller PDF files that are faster to send, upload or archive. The readability and quality of the content are preserved. For documents, presentations or digital records in particular, an optimized PDF file size is a big advantage.
