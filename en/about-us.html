About us

free-img-compressor.de did not start as a finished product or a big undertaking, but as an experiment – and as a personal learning path.

My name is Jörg Engelhaupt, I am a media designer and work at a retail company. There I create landing pages and work intensively on website layout. Since I have also worked in marketing in the past, over time I developed the wish not only to design a website but to run it entirely myself: from the technical implementation through hosting and visibility on Google to continuous further development.

I am not a web developer, but I have a great interest in building websites and smaller browser-based web applications. That is exactly why I deliberately chose a project that goes beyond a classic website. free-img-compressor.de serves as my playground for trying out new CMS systems and programming languages, understanding connections better and gaining practical experience.

The image compression web application was built with the support of ChatGPT. A central requirement for my site is transparency and data protection: The application is 100% browser-based. Images are processed directly in the browser and are not uploaded to a server. As soon as the image data has been downloaded, it is on your local computer and nowhere else.

The site is currently still at a very early stage. Both features and the user interface will evolve and change over the coming weeks and months as new insights and experiences flow into the project. Unfortunately, it can therefore happen from time to time that some features are temporarily unavailable.

The project is currently a private, hobby learning project. At the same time, it is open to development: what is an experiment today can grow into a long-term project over time. free-img-compressor.de is growing step by step – with curiosity, willingness to learn and the goal of providing a simple, fast and privacy-friendly tool for image compression.
