What does “PNG with transparency” mean?

A PNG image can contain transparent areas – for example in logos, icons or graphics. This transparency is controlled by the so-called alpha channel.

Incorrect compression often causes the following:

  • Transparent areas are displayed as white or black
  • The image is converted to a JPG
  • The quality visibly suffers

Our tool avoids exactly these problems and makes sure that transparency and image quality are preserved.

Typical use cases

  • Logos: Transparent backgrounds stay clean
  • Icons & UI elements: Perfect for apps and websites
  • Graphics for presentations: No distracting backgrounds
  • Web design: Faster loading times without quality loss

Especially on the web, it is crucial that PNG files are small but still stay pixel-perfect and transparent.

When should you not use PNG?

If your image does not need transparency (e.g. photos), a JPG is often the better choice, as the file size can be much smaller.

In that case you can also convert your PNG to JPG to save even more storage space.
